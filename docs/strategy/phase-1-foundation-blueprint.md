# Phase 1 Foundation Blueprint

Prepared on July 13, 2026

## 1. Phase 1 Goal

Turn the current temporary site into a credible platform foundation that supports:

- editorial publishing
- lead capture
- booking inquiries
- product sales later
- AdSense readiness later

Phase 1 is not about finishing everything.

Phase 1 is about building the structure that makes traffic and monetization possible.

## 2. Product Direction

The website should behave like:

`speaker brand + media platform + conversion funnel`

It should not behave like:

- a one-page celebrity site
- an ads-only blog
- a random quote archive

## 3. Core Audiences

Primary audience groups:

1. Students
2. Parents
3. Families
4. Event organizers

## 5. Homepage Structure

Recommended homepage section order:

1. Hero with positioning and CTA
2. Social proof row
3. Audience pathways
4. Featured talks or lessons
5. Topic hub cards
6. Free resource lead magnet
7. Latest articles
8. Programs and booking section
9. Testimonials
10. Newsletter or WhatsApp CTA
11. Footer with trust links

## 6. Required Page Templates

We should build reusable templates for:

1. Homepage
2. Topic hub page
3. Article page
4. Blog index page
5. Resource landing page
6. Booking page
7. Legal content page

## 7. Content Architecture

Each topic hub should include:

- one pillar page
- eight to fifteen supporting articles
- one lead magnet
- one conversion path

### Example Cluster

Topic hub: Parenting

- pillar page: how parents can better understand teenagers
- supporting posts: exam stress, communication mistakes, emotional support, comparisons, confidence
- resource: parenting mistakes PDF
- conversion: parenting workshop page

## 8. Design System Direction

Visual direction:

- warm editorial look
- premium but grounded
- mobile-first
- high readability
- calm motion

Recommended palette:

- canvas: `#f7f1e8`
- soft panel: `#f5ede2`
- earth accent: `#ae5831`
- forest accent: `#225f53`
- text: `#2d241d`

Typography direction:

- strong serif for section headlines
- clean sans-serif for body content
- strong spacing and readable line lengths

## 9. Monetization Sequence

Recommended order:

1. booking inquiries
2. lead magnets
3. ebooks and workbook bundles
4. affiliate modules
5. display ads after content maturity

## 10. Technical Direction

Current reality:

- the existing frontend is a temporary landing page
- it is not enough for SEO or content scaling

Required next direction:

- reusable page sections
- structured content data
- topic hubs
- article pages
- legal pages
- analytics-aware lead capture

## 11. Phase 1 Build Order

### Step 1

- finalize homepage direction
- finalize design system
- finalize top-level sitemap

### Step 2

- create page templates
- create content data structure
- create routing plan

### Step 3

- build About, Topics, Blog, Book Manish, Free Resources, Contact
- build legal pages

### Step 4

- prepare first 10 articles
- prepare first PDF landing page
- connect analytics and Search Console

## 12. Definition of Done for Phase 1

Phase 1 is successful when:

- the site no longer looks temporary
- the homepage communicates the business clearly
- the main content architecture exists
- lead capture is visible
- trust and legal structure is clear
- the next content and monetization steps are ready to execute
